The rules behind these changes

One horizontal bar per screen. Never two rows of sideways tabs or pickers stacked. A second choice becomes its own screen or a sheet.
No icon carries meaning alone. If it is not obvious, it gets a word: Map, Sort & filter, the dock labels.
One icon, one meaning. Envelope is inbox. Bubble is comments and chat. Sparkle is AI only.
One door to each place. Nothing lives in both the drawer and Settings. No second Home.
Plus makes the thing. A Link everywhere, the listing chooser on Marketplace. No question before the form unless there is no obvious default.
Hide what is empty. Zero counts, empty "Send to", role tabs with nothing in them.
Show a real price and a real time. Never "Paid", "From $0" or midnight for an unknown time.
